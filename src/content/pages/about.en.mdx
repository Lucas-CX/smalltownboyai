Smalltown Boy AI helps you put two people or pets into a 14-second vertical dance using separate photos. You do not need to film them together, write a prompt or find a motion-reference video.

## Your photos, your choice of roles

Choose one clear photo per subject. A takes the opening close-up reaction and B dances behind; swap the photos to swap their roles. The template provides the red-fence performance shown in our [complete sample](/smalltown-boy-sample).

Every new video is 720p. Review the price before generating. Packs are one-time purchases with no subscription, and unused credits do not expire. See [current prices](/pricing).

## See the result before deciding

The sample page pairs two fictional character images with their demonstration video. Its original song was added after generation; customer videos use generated audio. Watch the clip to check the routine, then read the [purchase details](/smalltown-boy-video-options) for output conditions and the 24-hour download window.
