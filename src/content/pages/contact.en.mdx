Email [support@smalltownboyai.video](mailto:support@smalltownboyai.video) for account, payment or privacy requests, including access, correction and deletion requests. You do not need to sign in or have an order to contact us.

For an existing video, you can also sign in and select **Get help** on an order in [My videos](/#orders). Include the order ID if available and a description of the issue; never include passwords or payment card information.

## What to include

- Account help: the email used to sign in and the action that failed.
- Payment help: your account email, payment ID and payment date, if available.
- Video help: the task ID and what went wrong with the result or progress.
- Privacy requests: describe the information or files you want us to review.

Support may ask for more information to locate the record. Do not send passwords, full card details or photos unless needed for the support request.
