Updated October 13, 2026.

To make this Smalltown Boy AI dance, select two separate photos with one person or pet in each. Put the opening reaction in A and the background dancer in B, review the price, then generate. The preset supplies the performance; you do not need a prompt, a photo together or a reference video.

## 1. Watch the version before choosing photos

Open the [full sample and its inputs](/smalltown-boy-sample). Smalltown Boy AI creates a 14-second, 9:16 video using the red-fence template. If you want a different routine or a longer sequence, compare the footage rather than relying on the song name.

The demonstration has the original song added after generation. Customer deliveries use generated audio, so sound may differ.

## 2. Choose one subject for each role

Use photos you have permission to transform. All identifiable people must be 18 or older and consent. A sharp face with even lighting is a better starting point than a group image, a tiny screenshot or a heavily filtered portrait. For pets, keep the face and distinctive fur markings visible. Read the [photo guide](/smalltown-boy-photo-guide) before selecting difficult crops.

In the [studio](/#studio), A takes the opening close-up and B begins behind. Use Swap roles before generating if you want the opposite order. Both photo slots are required. Choose a different subject for each role.

## 3. Review the total before generating

Sign in and review the price for one video or a larger pack. All new videos are 720p. Watching the sample is free; personalized generation requires credits. Packs are one-time purchases, with no subscription. See [current video prices](/pricing).

Return to the same tab after payment. An available photo draft is restored without starting generation or spending credits. Review your photos, quality, cost and photo permission, then click Generate. If the draft expired, select your photos again. Check My videos before submitting another task.

## 4. Inspect and download the finished MP4

Follow progress in My videos. Generation time depends on the queue; closing the page does not require starting a new task. Return using the same account.

Watch the close-ups and movement, check faces or paws, and listen to the audio before posting. Download within 24 hours of **video task creation**, not completion. If you need different music, choose audio suitable for your use in your editor or publishing platform.

## If the task is slow or the result is different

A processing task is not a confirmed failure. Check its status or select Get help before submitting another generation, which may consume more credits. Confirmed generation failures restore credits; this is different from a cash refund. A completed result may still vary in identity, motion or sound. Contact support about an unsatisfactory result rather than assuming it qualifies for an automatic refund.
