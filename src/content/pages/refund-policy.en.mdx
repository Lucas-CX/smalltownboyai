## Before checkout

Review the credit pack, currency and video cost before continuing. Credit packs are one-time purchases with no recurring subscription. Payments are processed through Stripe. Confirm the total and currency on the secure checkout page before paying.

## Failed generation

Credits consumed by a generation are restored after a confirmed failure. A delayed task or an uncertain provider response is checked before being marked failed. Do not submit another request while the existing task is being checked.

## A completed video that needs review

AI results can differ in likeness, clothing, movement and audio. A completed result that does not meet your expectations is different from a failed generation. Use **Get help** in [My videos](/#orders) and contact us with your order ID and a description of the issue.

## Refund requests

Restoring generation credits is not a cash refund. Cash refunds are not automatically issued by the tool. If you have a payment concern, contact support for a review of the order and payment record. Include your account email, payment or task ID, and a description of the issue. Support will review the record to determine the next step. Any mandatory consumer rights remain applicable.
