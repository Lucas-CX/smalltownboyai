## How to make your cats dance

1. **Pick one clear photo per cat.** Keep the face, ears and distinctive fur markings visible. Both slots need a photo; use distinct subjects. The [photo guide](/smalltown-boy-photo-guide) has framing tips.
2. **Choose who reacts first.** Upload both photos in the [studio](/#studio) and check the A/B order.
3. **Review the cost, then generate.** Sign in and confirm your photo permissions. After payment, an available draft is restored; you start generation yourself by clicking Generate.
4. **Check and save your clip.** Open My videos, review the result and audio, and download within **24 hours of video-task creation**, not completion.

Use photos you have permission to transform. If you include identifiable people, they must be 18 or older and consent. For the full workflow, see the [two-photo tutorial](/how-to-make-smalltown-boy-ai-video).
