## What matters when choosing a dance maker?

### Can I use the photos I already have?

With Smalltown Boy AI, choose one clear, authorized photo per person or pet. You do not need a photo of them together. A supplies the opening reaction and B the background dancer; swap their roles before generating.

### Do I need to learn prompts or find a motion reference?

No. Our red-fence routine and camera sequence are already set. You choose the two subjects instead of building a choreography workflow. The [sample page](/smalltown-boy-sample) shows the input images and full performance so you can see the routine before purchasing.

### What can I download?

A 14-second, 720p, vertical 9:16 MP4. We guarantee watermark-free downloads of completed videos, ready to save and share. Customer videos use AI-generated audio. The fictional cat-and-dog sample has the original song added after generation; that recording is not guaranteed in your output, and appearance and motion can vary.

No. Choose a one-time credit pack for 1, 3 or 5 generations, with no subscription. Unused credits do not expire, so you can use the remaining credits later. Review [current pack prices](/pricing) before generating; each new attempt uses credits. Confirmed generation failures restore credits.
