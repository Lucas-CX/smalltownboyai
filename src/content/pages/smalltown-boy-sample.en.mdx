## What you can inspect here

Compare the two input subjects with the close-ups and the moving figures. Watch the opening reaction, the partner dancing behind and the ending. Look for changes in face shape, markings, paws, clothing and movement. This lets you judge this specific sample instead of relying on a thumbnail.

The photos supply the identities. A fixed red-fence reference supplies the movement and timing; the studio does not ask you to write a prompt or upload that reference.

## What this example does and does not prove

This page pairs the two fictional character inputs with one published demonstration. It lets you inspect this clip's appearance, framing and choreography. The soundtrack was edited, so it does not demonstrate the audio of an unedited delivery. We have not published a multi-run success rate or measured a typical generation time from this example.

For a different subject, inspect the generated result before posting: check both identities, close-ups, limbs and sound. The [generator comparison guide](/smalltown-boy-generator-comparison) explains which requirements to compare when choosing a tool.
