Smalltown Boy AI turns two photos into a 14-second vertical duo dance using the red-fence template. Watch the [complete sample](/smalltown-boy-sample) to decide whether this is the routine you want.

## What does the purchase cover?

A one-time credit pack pays for generation attempts. The single pack covers one 720p generation; larger packs cover 3 or 5 at 720p. Other resolutions use different amounts of credits. Unused credits do not expire. See the [current USD prices and credit costs](/pricing).

Viewing the sample is free. Personalized generation requires credits, including when you generate the same subjects again.

## Check the result you need

- **Format:** vertical 9:16 MP4. Choose 480p, 720p or 1080p before generating.
- **Length:** the template is 14 seconds. It does not include every shot from a longer 21- or 22-second edit.
- **Movement:** fixed choreography and role order; no prompt or reference upload needed.
- **Music:** customer videos use AI-generated audio. The original song was added to our sample after generation; your video may sound different.
- **Watermark:** we do not guarantee watermark-free output.
- **Likeness:** faces, clothing and motion may change. A completed task is not a guarantee of a perfect match.

If you need the original recording, plan a separate editing step with audio suitable for your use. Contact us before buying if a specific output requirement is essential.

## What happens after payment?

Return to the studio in the same tab. A saved, confirmed photo draft resumes generation after payment verification if it is still available. If you only purchased credits or the draft expired, select your photos and confirm a generation. Check My videos before submitting again.

## Failures and downloads

Confirmed generation failures restore credits. Cash refunds and concerns about a completed result require a support review; see [Payments & Refunds](/refund-policy).

Photo and video access expires 24 hours after each **video task is created**, not after completion or credit purchase. Download the MP4 within that window.
