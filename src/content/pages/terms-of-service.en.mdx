## Using the service

Use the tool to create an AI video from two separate role photos, with one person or pet in each. Both roles are required. Review the available output setting and credit cost before submission. Credit packs are purchased through Stripe. Check the total and currency on the secure checkout page before paying.

## Permission to use photos

Upload only material you have permission to transform. All identifiable people must be adults aged 18 or older and agree to this AI transformation. Do not use the service to impersonate, harass or mislead others. You are responsible for obtaining permission; the upload consent does not independently verify it.

## AI-generated media

Video results are synthesized. Faces, hands, clothing, movement and audio can vary. Our template sample shows one result with the original song added after generation. The inspiration gallery is separate from our delivery samples. The template sample and does not guarantee exact likeness, choreography, generation time or the original trending song. Review the complete result before sharing.

## Credits and support

Credit packs have no recurring billing. Generation credits are restored after a confirmed failure. Cash refunds and completed-result concerns require a support review; see [Payments & Refunds](/refund-policy). Do not submit a second request while an existing task is being checked.
